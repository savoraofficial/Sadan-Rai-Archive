# Kirat Civilization — Evidence-Led Public Presentation

## Purpose
The public Kirat Civilization section is a visitor-first synthesis, not a dump of the 45-source research register.

## Public reading order
1. What is Kirat?
2. Where did the name Kirat/Kirāta come from?
3. How should the formation/development be reconstructed?
4. How old can we responsibly say it is?
5. What are the main evidence-bearing records?
6. What does each record actually establish?
7. Independent international cross-check
8. Research conclusion
9. Further research / unresolved questions

## Evidence discipline
- Direct archival objects, chronicles/traditions, and modern scholarship are visibly separated.
- No exact age-number is asserted unless directly supported.
- Ancient literary uses of Kirāta are not automatically equated with every modern Kiranti identity.
- Chronicle regnal totals are not presented as contemporary archaeological dating.
- Catalogue identity is not treated as proof of the entire content of an object.
- Restricted/copyrighted material is represented by lawful catalogue/reference targets, not fabricated public scans.
- “100% cross-check” means 100% treatment of the defined evidence inventory with explicit status; it does not mean every historical claim or every world archive is proven/exhaustively checked.
