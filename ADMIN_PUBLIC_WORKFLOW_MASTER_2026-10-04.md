# Sadan Rai — Public ↔ Admin Workflow Master

Date: 2026-10-04

## Locked principles
- The Admin console must mirror the public archive's information architecture so the owner can understand where every public section is managed.
- Public sections currently represented in the Home/navigation model: History & Civilization; Culture, Religion & Traditions; Places & Village Archives; Oral History; Research; Collections & Sources; and Sadan Rai — Original Research & New Findings.
- The public/admin relationship is additive. Existing research, evidence, source, author, oral-history, article, photo, video, category, translation, draft, published and revision workflows remain available.
- Sadan Rai — Original Research & New Findings remains a first-class primary research workspace and is not treated as automatically established historical fact.
- Places & Village Archives has a dedicated Admin workspace. Its current 11-subsection Malbase–1, Patlepani structure is surfaced in Admin and linked to research, oral history, evidence, photo and video workflows.
- Every Admin workspace must provide a clear route back to Workspace Home and a public-preview path where applicable.
- Nepali mode must use Nepali UI copy; English mode must use English UI copy. Original source titles, quotations, proper names and unavoidable technical terms may remain in their source language.
- Never delete existing working features, records, routes or research structure to implement this architecture.

## Original Research workflow
Field Finding → Original Record → Evidence → Existing Research Link → Independent Cross-check → Evidence Assessment → Researcher Analysis → Interpretation / Disputed Views → Final Finding → Public Presentation → Version History.

## Public presentation rule
The public Home now introduces Sadan Rai — Original Research as an ongoing, evidence-led research layer. It does not present an unverified finding as established fact.
