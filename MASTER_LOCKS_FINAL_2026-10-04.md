# SADAN RAI ARCHIVE — MASTER LOCKS 🔒

## LOCK 1 — Research Proximity & Visual Continuity
- `सदन राई — नयाँ खोज` / `Sadan Rai — Original Research & New Findings` remains a first-class research feature.
- The shared `सदन राई — अनुसन्धान कार्यप्रवाह` / `Sadan Rai — Research Workflow` remains visually close and in the same archival palette.
- Public and Admin presentation must preserve the same information architecture while allowing different density.

## LOCK 2 — Original Research Must Be Fully Functional
The Original Research/New Findings workspace is never decorative or a dead end.

Required trail:
**Field Finding → Original Observation → Original Evidence → Existing Research Link → Independent Cross-check → Evidence Assessment → Researcher Analysis → Conclusion → Publication → Version History**

A finding is not automatically a historical fact. Its status must remain evidence-bounded.

## LOCK 3 — Visitor Home Copy/Paste
- Public Home text must remain selectable and copyable.
- Visitor copy, paste, selection, context menu, print and ordinary browser actions are not blocked by CSS/JS protection.
- Nepali and English are both covered.
- Rights/attribution notices may remain, but must not block normal copying.

## LOCK 4 — International Admin Command Center & Workflow Structure
Admin is a professional editorial/research command center:

**Dashboard short → Workspace clear → Record detailed → Workflow sequential → Status visible → Version history safe**

### Core Admin workspaces
- Research & Sources
- Sadan Rai — Original Research & New Findings
- Places & Village Archives
- Oral History
- Research Library
- Evidence
- Sources & Scholarly Material
- Additional Archive
- Public Content CMS
- Categories / Translations / Drafts / Published / Revisions
- Dedicated 20-Section Kirat Research workspace

### Shared Research Workflow
1. Source / Lead
2. Original Observation
3. Evidence
4. Independent Cross-check
5. Evidence Assessment
6. Researcher Analysis
7. Conclusion / Further Research
8. Publication → Version History

Original Research adds the Field Finding and Existing Research Link layer while keeping the same quality and audit standard.

### Public ↔ Admin parity
Every major public archive section must have a corresponding Admin workspace. Where content is editable, the CMS workflow is:

**Create → Edit → Save Draft → Preview → Publish → Revision History**

### Preservation
No existing records, routes, 20 Kirat research sections, 45-file research library, uploads, Firebase collections, or working functionality may be removed or replaced by these locks. Changes are additive and narrowly scoped.
