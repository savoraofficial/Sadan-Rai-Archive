# Sadan Rai Archive — Kirat Evidence Master Register
## Evidence-first research lock — 2026-10-03

### Purpose
This register separates bibliographic references from actual evidence. A book title/page number alone is NOT treated as an evidence file.

### Evidence classes
- **A — Primary/near-primary documentary evidence:** inscription, original manuscript, dated historical document, archival record, contemporary/near-contemporary report.
- **B — Historical printed source:** old published work reproducing a historical account; exact page must be checked against the original edition.
- **C — Scholarly secondary analysis:** modern academic interpretation; useful for analysis/cross-check, not itself proof of the ancient claim.
- **D — Oral/community tradition:** evidence of a living tradition; not automatically proof of historical chronology.
- **E — Bibliographic lead:** reference identified, but original evidence still needs retrieval/rights review.

### Important legal rule
Public availability online does NOT automatically mean public-domain or permission to redistribute. Each evidence object must carry a rights/reuse status. Restricted/copyrighted scans are not to be republished merely because a URL exists.

- Source: *Recently Discovered Inscriptions of Licchavi Nepal*.
- Evidence type: **A/C** — inscription is primary evidence; article is modern scholarly analysis.
- Key location: article section “Licchavi Inscriptions”, p. 121 in the PDF pagination shown by the source.

## Missing-evidence rule
For every source above, the archive must show one of:
1. `ACTUAL_EVIDENCE_FILE_PRESENT`
2. `OFFICIAL_REPRODUCTION_AVAILABLE_RIGHTS_CHECK_REQUIRED`
3. `RESTRICTED_COPY — METADATA_AND_PAGE_REFERENCE_ONLY`
4. `ORIGINAL_NOT_RETRIEVED — OWNER_UPLOAD_REQUIRED`

A citation, URL, page number or bibliography entry alone must never be labelled `ACTUAL_EVIDENCE_FILE_PRESENT`.

## Cross-check rule
A modern statement about an ancient Kirata/Yalambar claim is not automatically independent corroboration. The archive must identify whether each source is:
- independent primary evidence,
- dependent quotation,
- later chronicle tradition,
- scholarly interpretation,
- oral/community tradition,
- or modern identity/religious reconstruction.

## Yalambar/Hang rule
Do not equate “Yalambar”, “Yalamba”, “Yellung”, “Yalunga”, “Hang”, or a Mahabharata character automatically. Each spelling/name must remain source-specific until an evidence-backed identification is established.
