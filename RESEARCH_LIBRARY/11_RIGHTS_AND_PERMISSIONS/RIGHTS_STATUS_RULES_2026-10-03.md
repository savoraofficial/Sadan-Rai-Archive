# Rights / Permission Rules

- `PUBLIC_DOMAIN/REUSE_PERMITTED`: reusable under the documented rights basis.
- `PERMISSION_GRANTED`: permission evidence is stored.
- `RESTRICTED/COPYRIGHT`: do not redistribute the source scan/PDF unless permitted.
- `RIGHTS_UNCLEAR`: keep citation/catalogue information and obtain clarification.
- `OWNER_UPLOAD_REQUIRED`: user/owner supplies the evidence file; archive does not fabricate it.

Digitised ≠ free to redistribute. A catalogue record or URL is not itself permission to copy the underlying scan.
