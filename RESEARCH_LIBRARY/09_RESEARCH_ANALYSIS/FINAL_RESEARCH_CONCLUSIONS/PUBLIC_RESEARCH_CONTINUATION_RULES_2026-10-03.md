# Public Research Continuation Rules

The public Research section may present the current cross-checked research status. Each public research record should show: title, subject/civilization, evidence status, source references, current conclusion, disputed/uncertain points, rights status where relevant, and update/version date.

New research is continuous: field visits, interviews with elders/knowledge holders, newly located books/manuscripts, archival documents, inscriptions, photographs, audio/video and other lawful evidence are added later. New evidence produces a new version and may revise a conclusion; it does not erase the earlier research history.

Other authors' copyrighted books/articles are sources and references, not automatically public full-text content. The archive publishes its own research synthesis with transparent attribution and evidence trails.
