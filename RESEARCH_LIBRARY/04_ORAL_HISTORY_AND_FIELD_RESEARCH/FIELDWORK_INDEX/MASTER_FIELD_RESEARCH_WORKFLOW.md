# Master Field & Oral History Workflow

1. Field Record ID
2. Place / village / municipality / district / country
3. Interview or observation date
4. Informant / source person
5. Language
6. Topic / claim
7. Exact question or observation context
8. Original testimony / field note
9. Audio / video / photograph / document upload
10. Transcript
11. Translation
12. Informant basis: personally seen / family tradition / community tradition / document / other
13. Consent and publication permission
14. Researcher analysis
15. Independent cross-check
16. Contradiction / uncertainty
17. Research status
18. Version history

Oral testimony is preserved as testimony. It is not automatically converted into historical fact.
