# MASTER FIELD CROSS-CHECK QUESTION MATRIX
## For continuation after current documentary/source audit

### Rule
Field research must test specific existing claims. It must not be used to manufacture agreement with books. Every answer is stored as testimony with informant attribution.

| Field topic | Existing evidence to test | Questions for elders/knowledge holders | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yalambar/Yellung/Ya'lamba | Chronicle/traditional accounts | What name is used locally? Who taught this story? How many generations? What place is associated with it? Are there different versions? | Video/audio, transcript, place photos, informant details |
| Kirat settlement | Hamilton early-19th-century account | Which ancestors are said to have settled here? From where? Which old place names survive? | Video, genealogy, place-name map/photo |
| Kirat/Kiranti group names | Hodgson and later scholarship | What self-name/ethnonym is used? What names are used by neighboring groups? | Recorded terminology, pronunciation, context |
| Mundhum | Academic and oral-tradition sources | Who performs/recites it? On what occasions? Who taught it? What variants exist? | Full/approved recording, transcript, performer details |
| Genealogy | Chemjong/Tanka Rai/local genealogies | Which ancestors are remembered? What written/oral genealogy exists? Where did the record come from? | Genealogy scan/photo, interview, independent informant |
| Old documents | Documenta Nepalica / local records | Does the family/community possess old land, tax, royal, ritual or genealogical documents? | Owner-approved photos/scans, metadata, rights |
| Place history | Historical maps/documents | What was this place called before? What structures/settlements existed? | GPS/place description, photos/video, oral accounts |
| Religious practice | British Library/Hodgson and scholarship | Which practices are remembered? What has changed? Which terms are old/local? | Ritual testimony, permitted photos/video, consent |
| Administrative history | Pallo/Majha Kirāta records | What old offices, taxes, land/custom terms are remembered? | Interview + local documents + comparison |

### Minimum informant record
- Full name or approved public name
- Approximate/actual age if consented
- Community/role
- Village/locality
- Ancestral locality
- Interview date/time/place
- Interviewer
- Camera/audio operator
- Consent for recording
- Consent for public archive or restricted use
- Language/dialect
- Relationship to the tradition
- How the informant learned the information

### Cross-check rule
For significant historical claims, seek more than one independent informant where feasible. Record agreement and disagreement explicitly. Do not delete contradictory testimony.
