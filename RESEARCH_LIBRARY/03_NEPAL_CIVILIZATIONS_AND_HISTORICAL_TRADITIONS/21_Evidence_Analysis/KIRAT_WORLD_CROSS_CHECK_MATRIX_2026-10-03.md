## Negative controls
- “Chronicle says” ≠ “contemporary history proves”.
- “Digitised” ≠ “free to redistribute”.
- Variant name ≠ proven identity equivalence.
- One modern thesis ≠ proof of an ancient claim.
- Missing page/folio numbers are never filled from memory.
