## B. Cross-check interpretation
These records establish that the catalogue contains specific Kiranta/Kiranti manuscript objects. They do **not by themselves establish** the historical truth of every statement that may appear inside those objects. Each claim must be tied to the exact folio/text and then cross-checked with independent evidence.

## C. Existing major evidence already retained
- Hamilton 1819: direct documentary evidence for Kirat/Kichak in eastern Nepal in the early 19th century; exact page targets already recorded.
- Documenta Nepalica: Pallo Kirāta corpus and RRC_0036_0252 appeal document.
- Hodgson papers: Limbu/Kiranti language, religion, culture, genealogy and manuscript holdings.
- Yalambar/Yellung: chronicle/traditional-historical status; exact ancient reign and Mahabharata identity remain unresolved.

## D. Negative controls
No page, folio, quotation, date, identity equivalence or “100% proof” is added unless the source itself supports it and the evidence object has been checked.
