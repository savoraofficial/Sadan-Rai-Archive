# Kirat / Kiranti — Current Cross-Checked Research Status

The current record supports the existence of Kirat/Kichak as a documented historical population/group in eastern Nepal in early nineteenth-century documentary evidence, especially Hamilton's 1819 account. Archival catalogues also document manuscript and documentary objects associated with Kiranta/Kiranti traditions, while Documenta Nepalica records provide primary-document evidence for Pallo Kirāta/Limbuvān and related historical administration.

These records do not by themselves prove every later claim about ancient chronology, a single uninterrupted Kirat dynasty, or specific legendary identities. Such claims remain separately classified according to their source and evidence status.

## Current scope
- documentary history
- chronicle and genealogy traditions
- archival manuscripts/documents
- language and cultural research
- Mundhum/oral tradition
- place and settlement history
- contradictions and unresolved claims
