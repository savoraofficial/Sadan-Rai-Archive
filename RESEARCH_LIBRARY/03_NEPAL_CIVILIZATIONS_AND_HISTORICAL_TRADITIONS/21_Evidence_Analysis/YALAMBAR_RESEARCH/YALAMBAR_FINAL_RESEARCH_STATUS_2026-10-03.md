# Yalambar — Final Research Status (2026-10-03)

## Current conclusion
Available evidence supports the conclusion that Yellung/Ya’lamba/Yalambar is preserved in Nepalese chronicle traditions as the founder or first ruler of the Kirata dynasty. Historical documentary evidence independently supports the existence of Kirat/Kichak communities in eastern Nepal in the early nineteenth century.

The exact date of Yalambar's reign, the precise historical identity represented by variant name forms, the identification of Yalambar with a specific Mahabharata character, and the claim that “Hang” is a securely established ancient official title are **not conclusively established by the contemporary evidence identified in this research pass**. These remain chronicle/traditional-historical or unresolved claims unless stronger primary evidence is identified.

## Negative controls
- Chronicle tradition is not automatically contemporary proof.
- A name variant is not automatically identity equivalence.
- A Mahabharata association is not automatically historical identification.
- Missing page/folio evidence is never filled from memory.
