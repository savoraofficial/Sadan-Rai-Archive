# Yalambar / Yellung / Ya’lamba — Cross-Check Status — 2026-10-03

2. Government `Nepal Parichaya` p.46 also presents a Yalung/Yalambar narrative, but it is an official compilation and must be cross-checked against its underlying chronicles.
3. Early nineteenth-century Hamilton provides independent documentary evidence for Kirat/Kichak communities in eastern Nepal, but **Hamilton is not direct evidence for Yalambar**.
4. The exact historical date/reign of Yalambar is not established by the contemporary documentary evidence identified in this pass.
5. Identification of Yalambar with a specific Mahabharata character is **not established** by the evidence currently recorded here.
6. “Hang = Yalambar’s verified ancient official title” remains **not established** unless a source directly supports it.
