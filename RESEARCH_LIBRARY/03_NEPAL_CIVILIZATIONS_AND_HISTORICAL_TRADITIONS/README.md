# Nepal: Civilizations & Historical Cultural Traditions

This directory is a research classification, not a claim that every folder represents a civilization in the same technical sense. Kingdoms, dynasties, peoples, cultural traditions, archaeological cultures, sites, and heritage records are separated where appropriate.

Evidence rule: do not treat an ethnic/community tradition, dynasty, archaeological site, or heritage designation as interchangeable with “civilization.” Record the source, date, evidence level, and scholarly terminology for each claim.
