## Evidence chain
**Claim → Source → Exact page/folio → Actual evidence object → Independent cross-check → Contradiction/uncertainty → Researcher analysis → Final research conclusion → Status/rights/version audit.**

## Core evidence statuses
- `DOCUMENTED`: directly documented in the identified source/evidence object.
- `SUPPORTED`: supported by independent sources; still source-bounded.
- `CHRONICLE_TRADITIONAL`: preserved in chronicles/genealogies/oral traditions, not automatically contemporary proof.
- `DISPUTED`: credible sources conflict or interpretation is contested.
- `UNVERIFIED`: source/claim identified but exact evidence not yet checked.
- `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`: further page/folio/field/archaeological work required.
- `RIGHTS_RESTRICTED`: evidence object exists but redistribution rights/access are limited or unclear.

## Universal scope
The structure is not Kirat-only. The same evidence discipline applies to Nepal, Himalayan, world civilizations, religions, cultures, traditions, oral history, places, inscriptions, archaeology and future archive subjects.
