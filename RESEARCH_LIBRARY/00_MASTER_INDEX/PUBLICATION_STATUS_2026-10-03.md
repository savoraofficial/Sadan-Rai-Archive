## Professional publication rule

This publication records the state of research available in the archive **as of 3 October 2026**. It does not represent a claim that every historical proposition in the worldwide literature has been conclusively proven. Each record retains its evidence status, source limitations, disagreements, unresolved questions, rights status, and further-research requirements.

New primary sources, archival records, oral histories, archaeological evidence, or scholarship can be added through versioned updates without deleting the v1.0 record.

## Publication layers

- Public research synthesis: published.
- Source and evidence trail: retained in the Research Library.
- Cross-check/researcher working notes: retained separately from public synthesis.
- Restricted/copyrighted evidence: represented by citation, catalogue/reference, rights status and official repository link; not redistributed as unrestricted copies.
- Firestore: remains the authoritative live CMS when the owner imports/publishes the prepared records there. The bundled application contains the same 12 records as the public fallback so the published snapshot is not lost when Firestore has no records yet.
