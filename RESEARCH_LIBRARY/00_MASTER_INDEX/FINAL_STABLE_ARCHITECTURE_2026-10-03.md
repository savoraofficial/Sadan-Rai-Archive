# FINAL STABLE RESEARCH LIBRARY ARCHITECTURE — 2026-10-03

## Locked purpose
This is the stable long-term research architecture for the Sadan Rai — History, Civilization & Culture Archive. The structure is universal and is not limited to Kirat. It supports civilizations, religions, cultures, traditions, local/place history, oral history, archaeology, inscriptions, manuscripts, comparative research and future subjects.

## Master rule
**Do not create a new top-level research structure for ordinary future growth. Add records, evidence objects, claims, interviews, files and versions inside the existing structure.** Existing folders/files are preserved.

## Research chain
Claim → Source → Exact page/folio → Actual evidence → Independent cross-check → Contradiction/uncertainty → Researcher analysis → Final research conclusion → Rights/status/version audit → Public publication when approved.

## Evidence semantics
- DOCUMENTED = directly documented by the identified source/evidence object.
- SUPPORTED = supported by independent sources, within their documented scope.
- CHRONICLE_TRADITIONAL = preserved in chronicles/genealogies/oral traditions; not automatically contemporary proof.
- DISPUTED = credible sources or interpretations conflict.
- UNVERIFIED = source/claim identified but exact evidence has not yet been checked.
- RESEARCH_REQUIRED = additional page/folio/field/archaeological work is needed.
- RIGHTS_RESTRICTED = evidence exists but access/reuse/redistribution is limited or unclear.

## Publication rule
The public archive may publish the current cross-checked research record, including its sources, evidence status, uncertainty and further-research notes. Publication does not convert an unresolved claim into established fact. New fieldwork or evidence can create a new version without deleting the prior version.

## File rule
Images, video, audio, PDFs and documents are uploaded directly through the archive's authenticated Firebase Storage workflow where permitted. External links are supporting references, not a substitute for lawful owner-uploaded evidence. Restricted/copyright material is not copied into the archive merely because it is digitised.
