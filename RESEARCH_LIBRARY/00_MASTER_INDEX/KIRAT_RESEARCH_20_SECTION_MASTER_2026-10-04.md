# Kirat Research — 20-Section Master Structure

Date: 2026-10-04

## Purpose
This structure expands the public Kirat research presentation from the original 8 sections to **20 research sections**. It does not delete the original 8; the 8 remain the first eight sections and the 12 additional sections extend the research architecture.

## Evidence rule
The 45-source cross-check remains the source corpus. A source can appear in multiple sections because one source may contain evidence relevant to multiple research questions. A section placement is **not** a claim that every statement in the source is proven. Exact page/folio evidence, access rights, and claim status remain authoritative.

## Final research workflow
Source → exact evidence → claim extraction → independent cross-check → evidence status → researcher analysis → final conclusion → public section → version history.

Where exact page/folio reading is pending, the section must retain a pending/further-research status rather than converting catalogue identity into historical proof.
