# Sadan Rai Archive — Universal 50-Section Master Structure

## Purpose
This is the universal architecture for **all civilizations, religions, peoples, cultures, kingdoms, states, languages, local histories, oral histories, archaeological subjects and comparative research** in the archive.

It is **not Kirat-only**.

## Master Rule
- 50 universal core sections are permanently supported.
- No existing section, source, evidence, category or research record is deleted to adopt this structure.
- A subject may leave a section empty or mark it `Not Applicable`, `No Evidence Identified`, `Evidence Pending`, or `Further Research Required`.
- Specialized sections can be added without changing or deleting the 50 core sections.
- The 50 sections are structural capabilities, not a claim that every subject has evidence in every category.
- Historical claims remain evidence-bounded; no automatic `100% true` status is allowed.

## Research evidence chain

`Source → Exact Page/Folio → What the Source Actually Says → Actual Evidence Object → Claim → Independent Cross-check → Evidence Assessment → Researcher Analysis → Final Research Conclusion → Public Section → Version History`

## Status discipline
Use explicit states such as:
- Bibliographically verified / page pending
- Page/folio verified
- Primary evidence identified
- Catalogue object verified / content pending
- Restricted evidence / owner-upload required
- Contradictory evidence
- Oral tradition
- Scholarly interpretation
- Further research required

Never convert a catalogue entry, modern retelling, oral tradition or unverified page into automatic historical fact.
