## Source-level notes

- Hamilton 1819 has exact page targets already extracted into the research library (pp. 2–3, 7–8, 133–134, 148–149).
- British Library EAP records establish archival object existence and catalogue metadata; restricted records are **not** treated as freely redistributable evidence.
- Documenta Nepalica records provide catalogue/document references; individual document text is required before making stronger historical claims.
- Nepal Parichaya is an official compilation and is not treated as independent ancient primary evidence.
- Mundhum scholarship is kept community- and author-specific rather than generalized across all Kiranti peoples.

## Negative controls

1. “Chronicle says” is not automatically “contemporary history proves.”
2. A catalogue record is not the same as a complete reading of every folio.
3. Digitised does not mean freely redistributable.
4. A later name-variant equivalence does not by itself prove historical identity.
5. One modern thesis or book does not independently prove an ancient claim.
6. Missing exact page/folio numbers are not filled from memory.

## Publication rule
The public archive may publish a source-bounded research conclusion, including uncertainty and disputed status. Admin-only cross-check notes remain separated from public records. New evidence should be added by version rather than replacing earlier records.
