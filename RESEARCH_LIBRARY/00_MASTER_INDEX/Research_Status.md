# Research Status

Use one status consistently:
- UNREVIEWED — collected but not assessed
- REVIEWING — source is being checked
- VERIFIED — bibliographic/source details checked and claim scope recorded
- PUBLISHED — approved for the public archive
- DISPUTED — credible sources disagree
- TRADITION — recorded as tradition/oral genealogy, not independently established
- REJECTED — unreliable, incorrect, or unsupported after review

Every important claim should record both what a source supports and what it does **not** prove.
