# Kirat Claim → Evidence → Research Flow

Every final research statement should be traceable through this chain:

1. **Research topic / claim**
2. **Source record** — author/informant, work, institution/archive, year, edition where applicable
3. **Exact location** — page, folio, chapter/section, catalogue identifier
4. **What the source actually says** — source-faithful transcription/summary
5. **Actual evidence attachment** — scan/photo/PDF/audio/video when rights and access permit
6. **Independent cross-check** — agreements, differences, contradictions
7. **Evidence status** — documented / partial / pending / disputed / not established
8. **Researcher analysis** — owner-controlled
9. **Final research conclusion** — owner-controlled, never auto-labelled as absolute truth
10. **Public section placement** — one or more of the 20 sections
11. **Version and change history**

## Important distinction
A source may support multiple topics, and one topic may require multiple independent sources. Therefore the archive intentionally uses a many-to-many source/section relationship.

## Rights
“Catalogue object verified” or “digitised” does not automatically mean the file can be redistributed. Restricted/copyrighted material remains a citation/evidence target until rights are cleared or the owner uploads a lawful copy.
