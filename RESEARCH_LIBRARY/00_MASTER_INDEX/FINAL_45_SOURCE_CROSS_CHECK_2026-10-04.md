## Evidence rule

- **Page-level / primary evidence**: an exact page/folio or direct scholarly edition is available and can support a narrowly worded statement.
- **Catalogue/object verified**: the institution confirms the object/work, but the historical proposition still requires reading the relevant page/folio.
- **Bibliographically verified**: the work/publication is securely identified, but exact claim pages are not yet extracted.
- **Page/work verification required**: do not quote or publish a stronger historical claim from the record until the original work/page/folio is resolved.
- **Restricted**: catalogue/object evidence can be cited, but scans/facsimiles are not redistributed without applicable permission.
