# Citation Style

## Books
Author, *Title*, Publisher, Year, p. X / pp. X–Y.

## Journal articles
Author, “Article Title,” *Journal*, volume/issue, year, pages, DOI/URL.

## Manuscripts / archival records
Institution, collection/shelfmark, title/description, date, folio/page if available.

## Digital sources
Institution/author, title, publication date if available, stable URL/DOI, access date.

## Research rule
Separate:
1. evidence,
2. source interpretation,
3. archive's synthesis/conclusion.

Do not convert a traditional genealogy or later ethnonym into a precise ancient date unless the evidence independently supports it.
