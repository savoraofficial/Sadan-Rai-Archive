# Admin Research Reader

The Editorial Console provides an owner-only **Full Research Reader** for the complete prepared research register.

## Access

1. Sign in at `/sadan-rai-editorial-login` with the archive owner account.
2. Open `/sadan-rai-editorial-console`.
3. Open **Research**.
4. Use the **eye / Read** action on any record.

## What the owner can read

- bilingual titles and explanations
- category, topic, status, workflow, version, date and rights
- author/informant and publication details
- chapter/section and exact page/folio fields
- original quotation and evidence description
- source URL when present
- research synthesis
- source-by-source research workflow
- cross-check agreements and differences
- evidence assessment
- researcher analysis
- final research conclusion
- attached evidence-file links when present

The reader is admin-only. Public visitors do not receive the private cross-check workspace.

The prepared publication register is also available in the admin reader before Firebase import. Firebase remains the authoritative live CMS once the owner publishes/imports the prepared records.
