# Research Cut-off — 3 October 2026

This archive's published research layer is a dated snapshot of research completed and sufficiently cross-checked up to **3 October 2026**.

**Publication rule:** only records with `researchStatus = verified` are published.

Research that needs additional page/folio verification, independent evidence, local verification, or future fieldwork remains in the Research Library but is not labelled as verified publication.

Future village visits, interviews, informants, local records, photographs, audio/video, manuscripts, inscriptions and archaeological observations will be added through new records and versioned updates.
